Privacy Policy

Arrow: Let Me Out · Last updated 16 November 2026

Arrow: Let Me Out is a puzzle game for Android and iOS. This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: your game progress stays on your device, and the only outside service in the game is Google AdMob, which shows the optional reward videos.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, hints, trophies, streak, settings and the name you type in Settings are saved only on your device. We have no server and no player accounts, so we never receive this information. Delete account in Settings erases it, and so does uninstalling the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers reward videos: you can choose to watch one to refill your hearts or get hints. These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location
  • device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with the ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy in Settings. On iOS, the game asks for permission before tracking. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Players who buy Remove ads or Premium see no ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details; we only learn which items you own, so the game can unlock them.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Emails to us

If you write to us through Write us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to answer you, and delete them on request.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Since we hold no game data about you, most requests can be handled in the game itself (Delete account, Ad privacy). For anything else, or for information Google holds, contact us or use Google's own tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
